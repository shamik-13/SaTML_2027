# `src/` audit — self-containment, minimality, notebook (8 Sep 2026)

Asked for after the paper froze: confirm `src/` is self-contained, holds only what the paper needs,
and that the notebook runs from cache.

## Method

Dependency facts were traced at runtime, not grepped. `builtins.open` was wrapped while running
`paper/make_appendix_tables.py`, `paper/make_figures_satml.py` and the gates, recording every read
under `src/lib/out/`. Module reachability was computed from the import graph rooted at `runner.py`'s
stage list, the notebook's module references and `figures.py`.

## Self-containment: confirmed, one defect fixed

Nothing in `src/` imports or reads from `proto/` (t70 agrees; the only two textual matches are
comments). No absolute paths outside the documented `LSPR_DIR` / `LSPR_CSV` / `LSPR_CACHE` /
`AIT_DIR` / `AIT_ZIP_DIR` overrides.

**Fixed:** `t17_T2_fullstream`, `t21c_H6_positions` and `t21d_H6_horizon` hard-coded
`/tmp/lspr_full.csv`, bypassing the `LSPR_DIR` override that `h_stream`/`h_meta` document and that the
other stages honour. Anyone keeping the derived CSVs anywhere else would have had those three stages
fail in `MODE="full"` while the rest worked. They now resolve the same way; the `/tmp` default is
unchanged, and all three were verified to resolve to a real file under `LSPR_DIR` and to `/tmp`
without it. This was invisible to cache-mode testing, which is why it survived this long.

## Minimality: already tight, nothing removable

- **62 modules in `lib/`, zero orphans.** Every one is reachable from `runner.py` (58 stages), the
  notebook (47 references) or `figures.py`.
- **59 artefacts in `lib/out/`.** 40 are read by the paper's table/figure builders or by the gates
  that verify the paper's numbers; 11 more are regenerated by the notebook's theory section, which
  re-derives every closed form the paper states.
- The remaining four (`t22_H1_H2`, `t28_P5`, `t29_compound_e`, `t43_E10`) have **no automated
  reader** but each backs a hand-transcribed claim, so all four stay. Verified two directly:
  `t22`'s `h1_summary.iforest` carries `margin` identical to the gradient-boosted detector and
  `tail = [0,0,0]`, which is App. D's "the same margin yet tail reach $0.000$ at all ten
  configurations"; `t29`'s `two_point` rows are all `b_star = 1.0`, which is App. I's "optimal
  e-to-e boosting factor ... is $b^{\star}=1$ by construction". **A trace of automated consumers is
  not a safe basis for deletion here** — prose claims have no consumer.

**Removed:** two `.DS_Store`, `lib/__pycache__`, and the eight AIT netflow zips (below).

## The AIT data (author decision)

`src/data/ait/` held 261 MB of `NF__<org>_netflows.zip`, which contradicted the paper's own Open
Science sentence that full-mode reproduction "requires downloading both records". Moved to
`.attic/src_data_ait/`; `src/` is now **17 MB**. What ships instead is what the paper says ships: the
dataset's extraction notebooks, `NF__label_info.txt` and `NF__README.md`. `MODE="paper"` still
reproduces every number with no data at all. Both fallbacks were tested: with an extracted `AIT_DIR`
cache `ensure_extracted()` finds all eight scenarios; with neither zips nor cache it raises a
`FileNotFoundError` naming the Zenodo record. `src/README.md`, `src/data/README.md` and the
`t51_R7_ait.py` header now say the zips are not redistributed. (15% of that payload was dead weight
anyway — the TCP-only pipeline reads `tcp_complete.csv`, and each zip also carried
`tcp_nocomplete.csv` and `udp_complete.csv`.)

## Notebook: works from cache

`MODE = "paper"` is the default. 43 code cells, **0 errors**, ~31–38 s, every cell produced output
(44 KB of it), theory section all `PASS`. Verified by content, not exit code. The README's exact
documented headless command (`nbformat` + `nbclient`, no nbconvert) also runs clean from `src/`.
Re-run after each change above, including after the zips were removed.

## Open finding: `proto/out/` has drifted from `src/lib/out/`

`src/lib/out/` is canonical (t70), but `proto/out/` still exists as an older snapshot: 18 artefacts
only in proto, 12 only in src, and **8 shared artefacts whose contents differ** — in every case the
src copy is newer.

This matters because **`t45` validates `docs/04` against `proto/out`**, while `t61`/`t65`/`t71`
validate the paper against `src/lib/out`. Three of the artefacts `t45` checks are among the eight
that differ. Re-running `t45` against the canonical store fails on `t49` and `t56` checks, including
"t56 reproduces t21c on every shared arm" (e.g. at 0.55: t21c 18 vs t56 3). So `docs/04`'s `t49`/`t56`
numbers were transcribed from the older proto runs and `t45` passes only because it reads that same
older store.

The **paper is unaffected** — it is built and verified against the canonical artefacts throughout,
and `proto/` does not ship. What is stale is the internal experiment record for those two stages.
Fixing it means re-deriving `docs/04`'s `t49`/`t56` passages from `src/lib/out` and repointing `t45`;
that is a separate job and is **not** done here.
